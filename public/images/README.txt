# Photo Scrapbook Folder

Place your special photos here:
- memory-1.jpg (e.g., The day we met or our first date)
- memory-2.jpg (e.g., A favorite trip or adventure together)
- memory-3.jpg (e.g., A sweet candid moment where you made me smile)
- memory-4.jpg (e.g., Stargazing, coffee date, or celebration)
- memory-5.jpg (e.g., Silly selfies that always warm my heart)
- memory-6.jpg (e.g., Holding hands or a quiet cozy evening)

You can customize the photo titles, captions, and dates easily in:
`src/data/personalization.ts`
