# Memory Videos Folder

Place your personal memory video files here:
- our-memory-1.mp4 (e.g., A special trip, laughing together, or a date night)
- our-memory-2.mp4 (e.g., A cute candid moment or surprise)
- our-memory-3.mp4 (e.g., A favorite place or anniversary moment)
- my-message.mp4 (A personal video of you speaking directly to her from your heart)

Tips:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC audio codec works on all browsers and phones).
- Keep video file sizes optimized (e.g. 5-30 MB) for smooth mobile playback.
- If you change filenames, you can easily update the paths in `src/data/personalization.ts`.
